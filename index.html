About

A browser for the New Hampshire 52 With a View list: what each hike involves, where to park, and what the summit looks like. It is independently maintained and not associated with any particular hiking group or site.

Check a primary source. Artificial intelligence was used to aggregate the sources below, so this page may contain errors. Always confirm anything that matters against a primary source, above all Ken MacGray's guidebook. Where this page and the book disagree, believe the book.

Be prepared. Before you head out, carry the American Hiking Society's ten essentials. Small mountains still get people into trouble.

Your ticks stay yours. There is no account and no tracking — checkmarks live in this browser only. Use Save my progress to turn them into a link you can bookmark or email yourself, which is the safest way to carry a multi-year list between phones and computers. To claim your patch, print the official checklist and post it with a stamped addressed envelope and $5 to the address on the form.

All of it is open. The source code is free to use under the MIT licence, and the data behind every peak, route, trailhead, town, landowner and federal name id is a single JSON file — the same one your browser just loaded — under the Open Database License, which asks only that you credit the sources below and pass the same freedom along. Take it and build something. Found a wrong turn or a stale trailhead? Tell us — corrections are the most useful thing you can send.

Reading the numbers. est. marks a distance or climb measured from OpenStreetMap trail geometry and USGS elevations rather than taken from a published source. A * on a view score means nobody has rated it yet, so it shows the list median. Where a peak's hiking name differs from its official one, the list's name is shown. Distances to trailheads are straight line, not driving distance.

Sources

The imagery, terrain, weather and radar at the end of this list are fetched by your browser when you open a peak, so those services see your IP address the way any website you visit would. Nothing is stored.
