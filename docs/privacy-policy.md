# Privacy Policy for 52 With a View

Effective date: 2026-08-13

52 With a View does not collect personal data from users.

## What the app stores

- Peak completion checkmarks are stored locally on your device using browser local storage.
- Saved progress links are generated in your browser and only shared if you choose to copy or send them.

## What the app does not do

- No account sign-in
- No analytics tracking
- No advertising SDKs
- No server-side storage of your checklist

## Network usage

The app fetches peak data JSON from this project's published files. Links to maps open Google Maps in your browser or map app.
